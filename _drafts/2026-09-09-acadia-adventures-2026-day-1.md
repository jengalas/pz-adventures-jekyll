Left at 2:30, got to Vernon Diner by 6:30. Traffic actually wasn't bad, and there weren't any major delays due to construction like last year. There was only one long delay right after we got back on the highway after eating, due to a "construction install" where a cop was blocking all traffic while the road crews set up cones and other equipment for their nighttime work. Unfortunately we were right near the front of the line, so I couldn't help thinking if we'd been just a minute or so earlier we would have avoided all of it (and had basically no other traffic on the road with us for miles). That took at least 20 minutes until they finished whatever they were setting up.

We arrived at the rest area by around 10:30pm so we had plenty of time for a good rest. All along our route we had been surrounded by rain, but we were lucky to escape anything heavy, really only encountering a few sprinkles here and there, and wet roads that indicated how closely we'd come to the heavy stuff. Even while resting in the car at the Kittery rest area, the worst of the drops were those falling off the branches of the pine tree above us. They fell with loud plops. The few showers that passed through overnight were light and, well, showery, and only lasted a few minutes each time.
