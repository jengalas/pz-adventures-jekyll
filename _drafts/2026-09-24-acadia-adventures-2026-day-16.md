Wanted to get up a little earlier, but we still got a pretty early start. Talked with Christopher before leaving (while R was getting our breakfast ready) and he set us up in advance for next year, assuming we can go. We left at 8:38am, and decided to take a slightly new route this time. Last night I was looking at the map just trying to get an idea where Mariaville is (the home of the people we met last night at Rosalie's) when I thought I'd check for what Google Maps says is actually the best route from Bar Harbor to Augusta. Just maybe, might it be Route 3 instead of having to go all the way up to I-395 and then spend like two more hours on I-95? Indeed, Route 3 is the shorter route! It's generally a few minutes faster, and 18 miles shorter. So we were all set to try it.

Just before reaching Ellsworth, we turned west onto Beechland Road and then took Route 230 north into town, right at the bridge, so we skipped all the congestion of Ellsworth itself. From there it was an easy drive along Routes 1 and 3 through some of our favorite towns (Bucksport, Belfast, Searsport) straight to Augusta. This was so much more pleasant than two more hours of interstate driving! We could've even stopped for another lobster roll, or something else to eat, along the way (but we didn't, this time). 

We're also planning to take the same route along Route 230 and Beechland Road next time upon our arrival, again so we can skip the congestion of Ellsworth. Rich had a feeling there was some kind of a shortcut like that, when we noticed so many people turning right after the bridge.

The rest of the drive was typical New England traffic, nothing too crazy, but just the usual stress. We stopped at the Gardiner, Maine rest area off I-95 (which they have apparently redone with some bizarre traffic rotaries - made very little sense to us at the time) and again at Westborough, Massachusetts on I-90. We avoided the crappy food at both but at Westborough the "Cheesy Street Grill" was offering samples of grilled cheese sandwiches with bacon, which were quite tasty.  We will keep this place in mind next time if we want something actually good on our way home!

I was keeping an eye on the traffic the whole way, and there were just a few spots with some delays, most notably the usual merge onto I-91, which is always backed up, at least by the time we get there.
